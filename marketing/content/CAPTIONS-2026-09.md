# Captions — September 2026 launch set

Every caption ships in Dari **and** Pashto. Post them as one caption containing
both (Dari first), or as two posts — never Dari only, which tells a Pashto
speaker the product is not for her.

## What is true as of 2026-09-18, and what is therefore not said

Checked against the database and the stores before writing, because the skill
requires every number in a post to be checked:

- **Two salons, both in Kabul.** So no caption names a second city, and none
  promises choice. The four-city line stays out until a city has several salons.
- **Android is installable today.** The iOS app is version 1.0 build 6, in
  review with Apple — so **no caption mentions the App Store or iPhone** until
  it is approved. `safebeauty.web.app/get` is the one link; it sends a person
  to the right place whatever their phone.
- **No salon, customer, price or booking in any image is real.** The screens
  are drawings. The rules forbid naming a real salon or showing a real booking,
  and there is no seeded demo to photograph.
- **No discount or offer is claimed.** Nothing is running.
