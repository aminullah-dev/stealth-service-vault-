# Content plan — autumn 2026

A body of work rather than a post: twenty-six pieces across six weeks, so the
channels have something to say on a day when nothing has shipped. Written once,
here, before any of it is rendered — because deciding what a month says while
looking at an empty Instagram grid produces a month of the same post.

## The facts everything here is written against

Re-checked 2026-09-18. Nothing in this plan may contradict them; if one of them
changes, the affected pieces change with it.

| Fact | Consequence for the copy |
|---|---|
| Two salons live, both Kabul | No second city. No "choose from dozens". Salon recruitment outweighs customer acquisition roughly 2:1. |
| Android installable now; iOS 1.0 (6) in review | One link, `safebeauty.web.app/get`. No App Store, no iPhone, no "on both stores" until Apple approves. |
| No promotion running | No discount, no giveaway, no "limited time". |
| No consented customer story yet | No testimonial, real or invented. A fabricated quote is the one thing that would cost the trust this product runs on. |
| Prices vary per salon | Never a platform-wide price. A number in an image is a drawn example and reads as one. |

## The three things worth saying

Everything below is one of these. A piece that is none of them does not ship.

1. **You do not have to phone anyone.** The whole product, for a customer, is
   that a booking takes no conversation, no explaining, no waiting on hold.
2. **An empty chair is money.** The whole product, for a salon, is that the
   hours nobody booked are hours she can sell.
3. **It stays between the two of you.** Not a slogan about secrecy — the
   ordinary, checkable fact that a booking is visible to the customer and her
   salon and to nobody else.

## Cadence

Six weeks, three posts a week, salon-first:

- **Mon** salon piece · **Wed** customer piece · **Sat** brand piece or a repost
  of the week's salon piece in the other language.
- Stories: the `story`-size render of whichever piece went out that day.
- WhatsApp channel: one post a week, the salon one, plus anything genuinely
  new (a salon joining, a city opening, an app release).

## What is deliberately not in this plan

- **A giveaway or a discount.** Both are the owner's call and cost money.
- **Anything naming a real salon or a real customer**, until one agrees in
  writing. Then it becomes the strongest piece here and jumps the queue.
- **A "four cities" claim** anywhere a customer can read it.
- **Paid promotion.** Budget is the owner's.
