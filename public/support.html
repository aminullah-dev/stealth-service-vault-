Frequently asked

  • I forgot my password — on the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot password?"
  • I want to delete my account — in the app: Support → Delete my account. Full guide: safebeauty.web.app/delete-account
  • I want to cancel a booking — from "My bookings", swipe the booking or open it.
  • My salon isn't on the app — register as a salon owner; your account activates after identity verification.
