Delete your account from the app

  1. Open the SafeBeauty app and sign in.
  2. Go to Support.
  3. Tap Delete my account at the bottom of the page.
  4. Read the warning and confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your name, phone number and email address
  • Identity verification data (tazkira, selfie, address, year of birth)
  • Your profile photo and any photos you uploaded
  • Your favourites, waitlist entries and notifications
  • Your sign-in account — you will no longer be able to log in

What is kept

Booking and payment records are retained in anonymised form: your name and phone number are stripped from them, but the record itself remains for the salons' accounting and for financial compliance. Reviews you wrote remain without your name attached.

Any upcoming bookings are cancelled automatically as part of deletion.

This action cannot be undone. If you can no longer access the app, email us from your registered address to request deletion: Aminhashemi979@gmail.com
We action requests within 30 days.
