## English (en-US)

Version 1.5 — a big update ✨
• Two-way ratings between customers and salons
• Book a specific stylist at multi-staff salons
• In-app chat with your salon and support while a booking is active
• Salon location on a map, distance and directions
• Step-by-step booking status tracking
• Advanced filters and sorting for salons
• Fingerprint sign-in
• Direct announcements from the SafeBeauty team
• A fresh, more polished look
• Fixes: one phone = one account, and password reset now finds your number
